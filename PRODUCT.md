# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two readers. The **catedrático** of Economía Global (UMG, sección 42026-1030-528-A) grades it against a rubric: UMG and course identification, exposition of the topic, application in Guatemala, group comments, and APA references. **Classmates** open the link during and after the class presentation, mostly on phones, and should be able to read a post start to finish without getting bored.

## Product Purpose

A group research blog on cryptocurrencies in the global economy, written by three students (Mishell, Zuis, Karen), each authoring their own posts. It must read as a real blog (chronological entries with author, date, reading time, tags, an author sidebar and archive) while still satisfying every rubric item. Success: the professor finds each rubric item without hunting, and the blog clearly stands apart from the Wix/Blogspot templates other groups submit.

## Brand Personality

Serious, approachable, local. Academic rigor (citations, references, order) delivered in a plain divulgation voice classmates enjoy reading. Visibly Guatemalan and UMG, not a generic template. Modern and visual through diagrams, tables and figures, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- Crypto exchange or trading "hype" pages: neon, black backgrounds, candlestick charts, investment-pitch tone.
- Generic free Wix/Blogspot templates: stock hero banners, default widgets, clip-art feel.
- Slide decks or report outlines: sections labeled A–E like a table of contents.

## Design Principles

1. **A blog first, a rubric second.** Posts, authors and dates lead; rubric items are reachable but never the page structure.
2. **Rigor you can see.** Citations, figures and references are typographically first-class, not footnote afterthoughts.
3. **Each author has a voice.** Every post clearly belongs to one student; the group speaks together only in the shared posts.
4. **Local identity, carried with restraint.** UMG crest and Guatemalan context appear where they mean something, not as wallpaper.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: body contrast ≥4.5:1, readable at phone width, keyboard navigable, all figures with alt text, motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
